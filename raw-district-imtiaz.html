Project Overview

raw-district-imtiaz is a landmark mixed-use development located in Downtown Jebel Ali on Sheikh Zayed Road. Designed as a vibrant urban ecosystem where living, working, creating, and socializing come together, the project features fully furnished residences, office spaces, retail destinations, co-working environments, and lifestyle-focused amenities within one integrated community. With contemporary architecture, smart home integration, and direct metro accessibility, RAW District is designed for modern professionals, entrepreneurs, creators, and investors seeking a future-ready lifestyle in Dubai.
