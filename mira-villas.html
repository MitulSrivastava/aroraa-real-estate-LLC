Timeless British Elegance & Waterfront Luxury

Experience an extraordinary blend of automotive-inspired elegance and waterfront living at Mira Villas by Bentley Home, an ultra-luxury residential development nestled in the prestigious District 11 of Mohammed Bin Rashid City (MBR City), Dubai. Designed in collaboration with the world-renowned Bentley Home, this exclusive collection of only 36 fully furnished villas and mansions represents a new benchmark in luxury real estate. Every residence showcases Bentley Home's signature craftsmanship, sophisticated interiors, premium finishes, and timeless architectural design. Surrounded by a crystal lagoon and lush landscaped gardens, Mira Villas offers an exceptional lifestyle where privacy, wellness, and luxury seamlessly come together.
