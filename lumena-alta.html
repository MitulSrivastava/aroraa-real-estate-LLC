Dubai's Gateway of Business Bay

LUMENA ALTA by OMNIYAT is Dubai's newest ultra-luxury commercial landmark, redefining the future of premium business environments at the gateway of Business Bay. Rising an impressive 380 metres, this iconic mixed-use tower seamlessly blends world-class office spaces, luxury hospitality, sophisticated architecture, and unparalleled connectivity into one extraordinary destination. Designed for visionary enterprises who seek an address that reflects prestige, ambition, and excellence.

The Developer

About OMNIYAT

Founded over two decades ago, OMNIYAT is one of Dubai's most prestigious luxury real estate developers, renowned for creating architectural masterpieces that redefine the city's skyline. The company collaborates with internationally acclaimed architects, designers, and artists to deliver landmark residential, hospitality, and commercial developments that blend innovation, craftsmanship, and timeless design.

With iconic projects across Dubai, OMNIYAT continues to set new benchmarks in luxury real estate by transforming visionary concepts into world-class destinations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about LUMENA ALTA by OMNIYAT

LUMENA ALTA is a 380-metre ultra-luxury mixed-use commercial tower by OMNIYAT located at the gateway of Business Bay, Dubai.

The development offers premium Grade-A office spaces, executive offices, corporate headquarters, and luxury commercial environments designed for global businesses.

It is strategically positioned in Business Bay with direct access to Sheikh Zayed Road, Business Bay Metro Station, Downtown Dubai, DIFC, and Dubai International Airport.

The project combines iconic architecture, a prime location, world-class amenities, direct metro connectivity, and OMNIYAT's reputation for delivering exceptional luxury developments, making it a compelling long-term investment.
