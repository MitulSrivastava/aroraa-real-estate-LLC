ORLA Infinity by OMNIYAT is one of Dubai's most exclusive ultra-luxury beachfront residences, located on the prestigious crescent of Palm Jumeirah. Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about ORLA Infinity by OMNIYAT

ORLA Infinity is an ultra-exclusive collection of only 20 beachfront duplex residences located on the crescent of Palm Jumeirah, Dubai. Developed by OMNIYAT and managed by Dorchester Collection, it represents the absolute peak of luxury coastal living.

The architecture is designed by the world-renowned firm Foster + Partners. The bespoke, high-end interiors are crafted by YODEZEEN, and the lush tropical landscape architecture is designed by ZED Collective.

Each of the 20 duplex residences features double-height ceilings, floor-to-ceiling glass facades, direct private lift access, designer kitchens with European appliances, and a private infinity pool (up to 18 metres long and 3 metres wide) on its expansive terrace.

Residents enjoy comprehensive, five-star hotel services including a 24/7 dedicated residence concierge, valet parking, doorman and porter services, housekeeping, common area maintenance, and à la carte lifestyle management (catering, florists, booking assistance).

Residents have exclusive access to a private beach club, infinity pool, indoor heated pool, fitness center, cycling studio, yoga room, luxury spa, bowling alley, kids play zone, co-working lounge, two private cinemas, and Dubai's first resident-only cigar salon.
