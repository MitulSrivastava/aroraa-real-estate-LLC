Project Overview

Palm Jebel Ali is Dubai’s newest ultra-luxury waterfront destination developed by Nakheel, designed as a world-class island community offering exclusive beachfront villas, resort-inspired living, and breathtaking coastal experiences. Spread across a stunning palm-shaped archipelago, the development features iconic architecture, private beaches, wellness resorts, entertainment hubs, yacht clubs, and luxury hospitality experiences. The project redefines elite waterfront living with contemporary villas crafted around space, light, privacy, and panoramic sea views.
