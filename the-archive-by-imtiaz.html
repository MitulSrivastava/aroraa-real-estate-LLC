Frequently Asked Questions About The Archive

Everything you need to know about The Archive by Imtiaz Developments in DLRC Dubai.

The Archive is a premium twin-tower residential development by Imtiaz Developments located in DLRC (Dubai Residence Complex) on Sheikh Mohammed Bin Zayed Road, Dubai. It features studios, 1-bedroom, 2-bedroom, and 3-bedroom apartments starting from AED 666,000. The project is distinguished by UAE's first signature residential library with over 10,000 books, 40+ world-class amenities, and a resort-style lifestyle concept.

The Archive offers four unit types: Studio apartments (360–426 sq. ft.) from AED 666,000, 1-bedroom apartments (546–1,234 sq. ft.) from AED 979,000, 2-bedroom apartments (1,022–1,686 sq. ft.) from AED 1.55 million, and 3-bedroom apartments (1,305–1,884 sq. ft.) from AED 1.9 million. All units come with premium finishes and modern layouts.

The Archive offers flexible developer-linked payment plans with 50% payable during the construction period and the remaining 50% on completion. Post-handover payment plans are also available. Contact our team for the latest installment schedules and personalized payment structures tailored to your investment goals.

The Archive is strategically located in DLRC (Dubai Residence Complex) directly on Sheikh Mohammed Bin Zayed Road. Key nearby landmarks include GEMS International School (3 minutes), Global Village (7 minutes), Dubai Outlet Mall (15 minutes), Downtown Dubai and Burj Khalifa (17 minutes), Dubai International Airport (17 minutes), and Al Maktoum International Airport (27 minutes).

The Archive features over 40 world-class amenities including UAE's first signature residential library with 10,000+ books, reading lounges, a writer's room, rooftop swimming pools, an outdoor cinema, mini golf course, state-of-the-art gymnasium, relaxation lounges, kids' play area, rooftop BBQ and lounge area, sun terraces, cabanas, a dedicated pets' area, and walkable green landscapes.

Imtiaz Developments is a trusted Dubai-based real estate developer with a proven legacy of delivering over 2,000 residential units across the UAE. Known for quality craftsmanship and innovative design, Imtiaz Developments focuses on creating signature communities that blend modern architecture with resort-style living experiences.
